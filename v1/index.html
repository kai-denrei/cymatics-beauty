what this is

v1 toy field — the membrane / Helmholtz superposition, not the Kirchhoff plate. It looks right and is physically wrong. v2 will replace this with a real scikit-fem biharmonic eigensolve on a free-edge plate.

field

f(x, y) = cos(nπx/L) · cos(mπy/L) − cos(mπx/L) · cos(nπy/L)

Nodal set is f = 0; particles flow down the gradient of |f|. m = n yields nothing — guarded.

synth tone

Frequency keyed to the mode: f ∝ √(m² + n²). This is the v1 cheat frequency, not a real plate eigenfrequency. Default muted; tap the icon in the scope panel to enable.

continuous vs. auto-cycle

∞ swaps to a new (m, n) once particles have settled on the current pattern (an EMA of mean particle speed crosses a threshold). ↻ swaps on a fixed timer. Either can be on; if both are on, settle wins.

physics distinctions kept honest

  • Plate (real Chladni, v2): 4th-order biharmonic, nodal lines.
  • Membrane (the v1 cheat): 2nd-order, drumhead.
  • Inverse Chladni: powder to antinodes via acoustic streaming. Out of scope.
  • Faraday waves: liquid surface, Mathieu equation. Out of scope.
