cymatics — v1

what this is

v1 toy field — the membrane / Helmholtz superposition, not the Kirchhoff plate. It looks right and is physically wrong. v2 will replace this with a real scikit-fem biharmonic eigensolve on a free-edge plate.

field

f(x, y) = cos(N·πx/L) · cos(M·πy/L) − cos(M·πx/L) · cos(N·πy/L)

Nodal set is f = 0; particles flow down the gradient of |f|.

synth tone

Audio frequency keyed to the mode: f ∝ √(M² + N²). This is the v1 cheat frequency, not a real plate eigenfrequency.

controls — glossary

M, N
Mode indices, integers 1…12. They set the spatial frequency of the field in x and y; higher values pack more nodal lines onto the plate. Must differ — the field vanishes when M = N.
J — jitter
Random thermal kick added to each particle every frame (0…1). Higher J makes the cloud wander; lower J makes particles snap to nodes faster but the pattern looks brittle.
S — settle speed
Multiplier on the gradient force pulling particles toward nodes (0.1…3). Higher = faster settling but more overshoot.
P — particles
Number of grains on the plate (2k…200k). Mobile defaults to 30k to hold 60 fps; desktop to 60k.
⏸︎ / ⏵︎
Pause or play the particle integrator. The field itself is not affected — only motion freezes.
∞ — continuous
Once particles have settled on the current (M, N), the cloud is reseeded to a uniform sprinkle and a fresh random (M, N) is picked. Reseeding before the new field engages amplifies the visual transition. Pressing < or > always overrides; the swap clock resets to give your choice ~1.8 s.
↻ — auto-cycle
Same reseed-then-swap, but on a fixed 4-second timer regardless of settle state. If both ∞ and ↻ are on, the settle trigger wins.
↺ — reseed
Re-randomize every particle's position. Useful when the cloud has clumped or you want a fresh start without touching the field.
♪
Toggle the synth tone. Default muted (browser policy — the AudioContext only spins up on your first unmute click).

physics distinctions kept honest

  • Plate (real Chladni, v2): 4th-order biharmonic, nodal lines.
  • Membrane (the v1 cheat): 2nd-order, drumhead.
  • Inverse Chladni: powder to antinodes via acoustic streaming. Out of scope.
  • Faraday waves: liquid surface, Mathieu equation. Out of scope.
